Project Description

The Client was experiencing inconsistent sales performance across regions and stores, declining margins in some categories and uncertainty around discounting and customer loyalty. The project analysed FY2024 sales data to identify performance trends and provide recommendations to support revenue optimisation and business planning.

Business Need

The Client needed better visibility into regional and store performance, product profitability, discount effectiveness, customer loyalty and return patterns. Management required reliable analysis to identify underperforming areas and make more informed commercial decisions.

Approach

We assessed and validated the quality of the available sales data before cleaning and enriching the dataset. We then created calculated fields, analysed sales and profitability trends, compared regions, stores and categories, reviewed customer loyalty and discount patterns, and developed dashboard outputs to communicate actionable findings.

Responsibilities

  • Assessed and validated sales data quality.
  • Reviewed missing customer, loyalty and product cost information.
  • Analysed sales performance across regions and stores.
  • Evaluated product category performance and profitability.
  • Analysed discount effectiveness and return patterns.
  • Reviewed customer loyalty performance.
  • Identified monthly sales trends and seasonal fluctuations.
  • Developed dashboard-based insights and recommendations.
  • Translated findings into practical revenue optimisation opportunities.

Projected Achievements

Key analytical findings supporting the recommendations: the dataset contained $111,467.05 in net sales across 720 orders, with a 45% gross margin and 3% return rate. The Midwest recorded the highest regional sales, the South the lowest, Grocery was the highest-performing category, September was the strongest sales month and May was the lowest.

  • Target a 10–15% improvement in sales performance across underperforming regions and stores through targeted operational and commercial reviews.
  • Target a 5–10% increase in repeat-purchase activity by strengthening the loyalty programme and encouraging movement into higher-value loyalty tiers.
  • Improve promotional efficiency by using discount-performance analysis to protect margins while maintaining sales growth.
  • Target a 0.5–1 percentage-point reduction in return rate through continued monitoring of return patterns and product-level issues.
  • Improve management visibility through regular KPI monitoring covering sales, regional performance, customer loyalty, margins and returns.
  • Improve the reliability of future business decisions through stronger data-quality controls and standardised data-entry processes.
