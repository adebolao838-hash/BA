Project Description

The Client was experiencing concerns around employee turnover, inconsistent performance tracking and unclear salary structures across departments. The project analysed workforce data to provide leadership with a clearer view of workforce health and identify areas requiring targeted intervention.

Business Need

The Client needed a consolidated, data-driven view of workforce performance to understand where employee turnover was concentrated, whether performance was linked to employee exits, how salary levels varied across departments and where attendance issues required further investigation.

Approach

We used SQL to analyse workforce data across departments, comparing employee turnover, performance scores, salary levels and absence patterns. The findings were then translated into focused management priorities and recommendations covering employee retention, performance support, compensation review and attendance monitoring.

Responsibilities

  • Analysed workforce data using SQL.
  • Investigated employee turnover across Engineering, Marketing, HR and Sales.
  • Compared average performance scores between employees who left and those who stayed.
  • Reviewed salary differences across departments.
  • Analysed employee absence patterns.
  • Identified the strongest workforce signals and areas requiring further investigation.
  • Translated analytical findings into actionable workforce recommendations.
  • Supported the development of a structured workforce action plan.

Projected Achievements

Key analytical findings supporting the recommendations: Engineering and Marketing recorded the highest number of leavers, with 18 of 28 recorded leavers across the four departments. Employees who left had an average performance score of 4.12 compared with 4.07 for those who stayed, while Sales recorded the lowest departmental average performance score at 4.00.

  • Target a 10–15% reduction in employee turnover within Engineering and Marketing through targeted retention reviews and employee feedback analysis.
  • Support a 5–10% improvement in Sales performance indicators through structured coaching, capability reviews and performance-support initiatives.
  • Improve workforce decision-making by introducing regular monitoring of turnover, performance, salary and absence KPIs.
  • Reduce avoidable attendance issues through a structured follow-up process and earlier management intervention.
  • Provide leadership with clearer evidence for workforce planning and compensation decisions, reducing reliance on assumptions when addressing retention and performance issues.
