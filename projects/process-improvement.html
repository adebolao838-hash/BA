Project Description

Process analysis for university student services, identifying bottlenecks, requirements and opportunities for improvement. The case study documents the problem, analysis approach, outputs and resulting recommendations in a format that can be reviewed by a recruiter or hiring manager.

Business Need

Define the business problem clearly, understand stakeholder needs and establish what success should look like.

Approach

Gather and structure information, analyse the current state, identify gaps and translate findings into practical outputs.

Responsibilities

  • Defined and structured the business problem and project objectives.
  • Identified relevant stakeholders, information needs and requirements.
  • Analysed processes, data or customer pain points depending on the project scope.
  • Created business analysis artefacts and documented findings.
  • Produced clear insights and recommendations based on available evidence.
  • Presented outputs in a format suitable for stakeholder review.

Achievements

  • Converted an open-ended business problem into a structured analysis.
  • Produced documented outputs that make the reasoning and recommendations easy to follow.
  • Demonstrated practical use of business analysis and data analysis techniques.
  • Identified opportunities for process, customer or performance improvement.
